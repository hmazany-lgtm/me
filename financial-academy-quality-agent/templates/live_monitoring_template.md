**Event Type Definitions**:
- `LOW_ENGAGEMENT`: Active participation below threshold for a full window
- `TECHNICAL`: Platform, audio, video, or connectivity issue
- `CONTENT`: Trainer deviates from approved content or omits required disclosure
- `PACING`: Module running > 15% over or under scheduled time
- `OTHER`: Any other notable quality incident
